Gmail Pod Access - Privacy Policy

Gmail Pod Access is a private tool that runs only on Russell Rutledge's own computer, for his family's own Google accounts.

It reads and changes Gmail and Google Drive data only when the signed-in account owner runs it. The sign-in token stays on that one computer. The tool sends data only to Google's own APIs and shares it with no one.

To remove its access, open your Google Account permissions page and remove Gmail Pod Access.
